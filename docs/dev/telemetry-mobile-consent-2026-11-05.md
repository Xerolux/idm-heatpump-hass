# Mobile consent copy — private build 0.26.0-b8

The operator's mobile screenshot showed that the consent text was difficult to
read. Both steps now use plain-language first-layer summaries and the project
identity Xerolux. The full legal name and contact remain in the linked full
privacy notice. No data collection, consent policy, default, schedule or plant
behavior changed; existing consent is not resubmitted for this presentation edit.

The German firmware summary is approximately 85 words instead of 169; telemetry
is 99 instead of 259. Firmware starts with what the sensor tells the user, then
identifies the minimal metadata, independent opt-in, community provenance and
withdrawal. Telemetry identifies the purpose, unchanged myIDM assignment,
non-anonymous reports, plant-only scope, daily reports, active retention and
possible additional backup retention. These material points remain visible.
Both steps link directly to full provider/contact/data-rights information.

The labels are short affirmative actions: Allow firmware queries and Share plant
reports, with equivalent German labels. Neither existing opt-in logic nor its
default was changed. Full metadata lists, IP/rate handling, polling jitter,
Recorder behavior, backup mechanics and the erasure journal remain in the linked
notice rather than overwhelming the short dialog.

Targeted consent, translation, telemetry and firmware tests passed (287 cases),
with additional documentation/style checks passing after the final copy edit.
The private HA package is backed up before installation. Live verification opens
both flows without saving and checks the actual English/German frontend resources.
It confirms short branded descriptions and privacy links without publishing the
myIDM ID. Entry options, connection settings and the pending daily slot are checked
against the pre-install backup. No public release, tag, push or actuator command.

The layered approach follows the [EDPB transparency guidance](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/system/files/2023-09/wp260rev01_en.pdf):
clear purpose, identifiable controller, key impact and rights in the first layer,
with accessible full information. This copy edit is not a legal certification.
