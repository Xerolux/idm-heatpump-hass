# Release Candidate Evidence: 0.21.3-b1

Status: `BLOCKED` for stable promotion; prerelease validation is in progress.

## Scope

The candidate fixes independently reproduced external-power forwarding races:
sensor events cancelled an active batch, continuous bursts postponed its initial
write, and periodic/event-driven passes could interleave. No register addresses,
encoding, cooldowns or controller setpoints change.

Regression tests cover an event during a blocked first write, completion of the
remaining SOC write, a follow-up pass using the new source state, and serialized
concurrent batches. Unloading awaits cancellation of the owned event task.

## Hardware observations before installing this candidate

Owner-authorized GLT measurement forwarding was configured with production,
house consumption, surplus, battery discharge and fractional battery SOC.
Navigator 10 firmware T_NAV10_20.24-1580 reported a battery in all 35 sampled
energy-flow frames over 42.4 seconds; direct Modbus SOC stayed valid throughout.
Power and SOC were read back directly from the controller, not inferred from
optimistic HA entity states. A temporarily unavailable inverter rejoined the
partial aggregate when it recovered.

These observations used the preceding stable integration. They do not establish
that this candidate fixes the controller-display behavior reported in #493 on
older firmware, nor constitute a long-term soak. That issue remains open.

## Installed candidate observation

HACS installed the exact prerelease, HA configuration validation passed and HA
was restarted. Diagnostics confirmed the running integration version and enabled
five-register GLT mapping. Direct device observations then showed a battery in
all 35 energy-flow frames over 44.8 seconds, with valid SOC changing from 89 to
88 percent and refreshed battery-discharge/house measurements. Surplus was zero
during battery discharge. All mapped IDM measurement entities were enabled;
the diagnostic last-write-error field was empty. This remains a short field
observation, not the required seven-day soak or proof for the older #493 firmware.

## Stable promotion

Seven consecutive 24-hour soak periods on the unchanged candidate, complete
release smoke evidence and maintainer sign-off are still required. No soak or
maintainer signature is claimed by this document.
