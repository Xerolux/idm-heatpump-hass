## What this beta explicitly verifies live

1. The six per-mode COP sensors (`energy_cop_heating_*`,
   `energy_cop_dhw_*`) register on a real plant and fill separately
   during pure heating and pure hot-water intervals (counters start at
   zero; the first DHW charge and heating cycle of the observation window
   are the interesting transitions).
2. The DHW recommendation window times render in the configured Home
   Assistant time zone (Europe/Berlin on the reference plant), not UTC.
3. The one-time building-model relearn (storage version 2 → 3): heat
   loss and thermal inertia become unavailable after the update and
   relearn from scratch; recommendations must not error.
4. Advisor state survives the upgrade (active recommendations, history,
   quality counters) with the additive `refreshed_at` field parsed from
   the b9-era store.
5. Store-write throttling: no unexpected growth of write activity in the
   logs; recommendation events unchanged.
