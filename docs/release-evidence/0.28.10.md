# Release evidence for 0.28.10

[Issue #757](https://github.com/Xerolux/idm-heatpump-hass/issues/757) reported short compressor cycles on an AERO ALM 6-15 with a Navigator 10 on firmware T_NAV10_20.24-1580. One week of history from a Navigator 10 on the same model and firmware (integration 0.28.9, 2026-11-20 to 2026-11-27) is the field evidence: at each of 47 defrosts the compressor status dropped when the operating mode switched to defrost, returned after a median of 0.9 minutes, dropped again after a defrost run of 3.3 to 7.2 minutes, and heating resumed about 3.3 minutes later. The tracker counted every defrost run as a compressor cycle and start: 133 starts, and 47 of 48 cycles under 15 minutes were defrost runs. Replaying that week through the new rule gives 86 starts, 47 defrosts and one cycle under 15 minutes, a real 13.7-minute heating run; that replay was a local check on the exported history, not part of the test suite. Regression tests replay the sequence sample by sample, a stop one sample after the mode leaves defrost, a mode reading that flickers back into defrost, a restart inside a defrost run, a run that continues from the defrost into heating, and the one-time reset of durations recorded before this version.

On Home Assistant 2026.11.0 the integration passed both coverage gates (98.37% overall and 100% for the config flow) with 3,776 unit tests and all 16 real-Home-Assistant tests. Two POSIX-only tests were skipped on Windows. Strict mypy and ruff check and format (0.17.6) were clean. The minimum supported Home Assistant remains 2026.11.0 and the API pin remains 2.18.0.

This is a same-day patch to the operating analysis only; no entity, option or register access changed. Verified on one controller model and firmware; confirmation on the reporting installation and the seven-day soak remain open.

Live verification on 2026-11-27: 0.28.10 was installed on the Navigator 10 that supplied the field evidence (update at 19:01 UTC, Home Assistant 2026.12.0b2). After the restart the short-cycle sensor, the average cycle runtime and the shortening health check were unavailable as designed, the start counters kept their values (22 starts and 10 defrosts for the day), and the running heating cycle continued from its start at 19:21 local time. The next defrost started at 21:00:50 local time: compressor status off, on in defrost for 5.9 minutes, off for 3.5 minutes, heating resumed at 21:10:15. Cycles today went from 22 to 23 — one start, the restart after the defrost, where the previous rule counted two — the last completed cycle is the 99.3-minute heating run that ended at the defrost start, the short-cycle sensor stayed off and the average stayed at 99.3 minutes. Confirmation on the installation that reported [#757](https://github.com/Xerolux/idm-heatpump-hass/issues/757) is still pending.
