# Release evidence for 0.28.10

[Issue #757](https://github.com/Xerolux/idm-heatpump-hass/issues/757) reported short compressor cycles on an AERO ALM 6-15 with a Navigator 10 on firmware T_NAV10_20.24-1580. One week of history from a Navigator 10 on the same model and firmware (integration 0.28.9, 2026-11-20 to 2026-11-27) is the field evidence: at each of 47 defrosts the compressor status dropped when the operating mode switched to defrost, returned after a median of 0.9 minutes, dropped again after a defrost run of 3.3 to 7.2 minutes, and heating resumed about 3.3 minutes later. The tracker counted every defrost run as a compressor cycle and start: 133 starts, and 47 of 48 cycles under 15 minutes were defrost runs. Replaying that week through the new rule gives 86 starts, 47 defrosts and one cycle under 15 minutes, a real 13.7-minute heating run. A regression test replays the recorded sequence, a restart inside a defrost run, a run that continues from the defrost into heating, and the one-time reset of durations recorded before this version.

On Home Assistant 2026.11.0 the integration passed both coverage gates (98.36% overall and 100% for the config flow) with 3,775 unit tests and all 16 real-Home-Assistant tests. Two POSIX-only tests were skipped on Windows. Strict mypy and ruff check and format (0.17.6) were clean. The minimum supported Home Assistant remains 2026.11.0 and the API pin remains 2.18.0.

This is a same-day patch to the operating analysis only; no entity, option or register access changed. Verified on one controller model and firmware; confirmation on the reporting installation and the seven-day soak remain open.
