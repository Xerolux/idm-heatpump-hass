tests, not by a forced physical cycle.

## Other observed HA findings

The following existed before this update and remain outside this integration
release: KNX group-read timeouts during startup, a service referencing a missing
climate entity, a weather sensor statistics-unit mismatch, SolarEdge cumulative
energy readings lower than prior values, and a Google state-reporting warning.
The baseline also contained intermittent SolarEdge communication warnings and
one slow advisor-state update. No configuration or statistics were altered
while investigating them. A single tmodbus unexpected-response warning occurred
during shutdown of the preceding installation; it is not evidence of a b7
polling regression.
