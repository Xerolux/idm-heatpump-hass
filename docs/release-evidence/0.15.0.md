`BLOCKED` is the accurate state of this record, and the stable tag was cut
anyway. That is a deliberate maintainer decision taken at release time, not an
oversight, and it is written down here rather than hidden behind a green
checkmark. Two gates from `docs/RELEASE_PROCESS.md` are open:

record and the documentation that references the version.
