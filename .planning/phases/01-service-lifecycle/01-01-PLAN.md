<objective>
Decouple the four domain-wide IDM services from the per-config-entry lifecycle so they survive
entry reload/unload/remove (GitHub issue #171).

Purpose: After an options change or entry reload, users lose `set_external_climate`,
`set_system_mode`, `acknowledge_errors`, and `write_register` until a HA core restart. The root
cause is that `async_unload_entry` removes domain-scoped services that `async_setup` registered.
Output: Services outlive any single entry; translated errors cover the no-entry case.
</objective>

<interfaces>
Domain services are global HA services under the `idm_heatpump` domain. They are registered once
per domain load (async_setup) and must not be torn down by per-entry unload. The active config
entry is resolved only at call time in `_get_coordinator` (services.py:86), so removing the
unload-time teardown changes no call-time resolution. DHW-Boost services are out of scope: they
register from button.py:59 and unload from button.py:164, keyed by `unloading_entry_id`.
</interfaces>
